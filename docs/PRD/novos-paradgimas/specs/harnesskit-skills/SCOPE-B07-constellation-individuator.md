# SCOPE B07 — `constellation-individuator` (Level 5)

> **Track:** B — New harness-kit skills · **Station:** REFLEXION, genesis form (ADR_V1 §4, Level 5)
> **Status:** REVIEW · **Mark:** [A/E] — **this scope is deliberately last and deliberately unrefined** · **Deps:** A09, B05 · **Codes in:** harness-kit (not in this implementation cycle)
> **Provenance:** ADR_V1 §4 (Individuating); PRD_V1 §3.13; PRD_V2 §5 (Level-5 gate rule)

## Problem

Recurring trace patterns with no owning archetype are the system differentiating a new organ from its own lived experience (ADR_V1 Level 5). This is the union creating creators — the most powerful and most inflation-prone capability in the entire line. It must be admitted only after Levels 3–4 are proven (a system that cannot be corrected must not be allowed to grow; a system that cannot be audited must not be allowed to evolve — ADR_V1 §5.5).

## Scope

**In:**
- Skill definition implementing pattern mining over factual traces (A09/B05): recurring session shapes with no owning contract surface as **candidate new-skill proposals**.
- Each candidate is a typed proposal containing: the recurring pattern (with trace refs), the proposed contract draft (B06 format), the territory-overlap analysis against all existing contracts (A02 report), the non-regression plan (B08 feed), and the budget forecast.
- **One targeted candidate per run** — the indivuation is one organ at a time (the meta-harness lineage rule: one evidence-backed improvement, never a grand redesign).
- Promotion is human-only: the candidate parks in `WAITING_HUMAN`; no path exists from mining to admission without the subject (ADR_V1 §5.6: the unbound Ouroboros).

**Out:**
- Admission of new skills (B08 gates it).
- Any modification of existing skills (that is Level-4 evolution, a separate later scope, deliberately excluded here — individuation births; it does not edit).
- Automated deprecation/death of skills (ADR_V1 §7.2 open question — remains open).

## Acceptance criteria (testable in isolation)

1. **Given** N ≥ threshold recurring trace patterns, **When** the individuator runs, **Then** it surfaces at most one candidate with all typed fields (pattern, draft contract, overlap analysis, non-regression plan, budget forecast) — zero candidates is a valid outcome when no pattern clears the threshold.
2. **Given** a candidate whose territory overlaps an existing contract, **When** the proposal is emitted, **Then** the overlap analysis is attached and the candidate does not proceed to B08 until resolved or explicitly justified.
3. **Given** any candidate, **When** promotion is attempted without operator approval, **Then** refusal — the log shows no automated admission path (reachability test).
4. **Given** the individuator's own budget, **When** exhausted, **Then** escalation with partial findings — never a truncated sweep presented as complete.
5. **Given** the Level gate (ADR_V1 §5.5), **When** Track A judgment scopes (A07, A08, A10) are not admitted, **Then** this skill refuses to run at all — the scope encodes its own prerequisite.

## Open questions (this scope is [A] — these are the reason)

- What is the recurrence threshold? Found by experiment, never imposed a priori.
- Does a candidate organ get a *probation horizon* (operate in restricted mode, A01 rule 2, for K sessions) before full admission? Proposal: yes — restricted mode is the natural amniotic fluid for a new archetype.
- Is the individuator allowed to propose its own retirement (theminer mining the pattern of its own obsolescence)? Genuinely open — kept visibly open per the Axiom of Provenance.
