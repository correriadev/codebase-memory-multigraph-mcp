# PRD V2 — The Harness: Engine of Governed Creation and Governed Memory

> **Product:** The Harness, synthesis edition — the universal pattern by which raw generative potential becomes reliable, verified, humanly-governed software **and** by which what was created is remembered as governed, contestable, revocable belief
> **Analyst:** C. G. Jung, depth psychologist of the engineering collective
> **Status:** Synthesis level. V1 described the engine of creation in the abstract. V2 incorporates three corrections delivered by a reference incarnation — a system that governs the truth of what was created (an admitted-claim memory with provenance, recall, and a scoped human root). Where V1 and the reference disagree, this document sides with the reference.
> **Lineage:** PRD (concrete) → PRD_V1 (abstraction) → **PRD_V2 (synthesis)**

---

## 1. Vision Statement

V1 described the Harness as a temenos for **creation**: the bounded enclosure within which chaos becomes verified form. The reference incarnation revealed that this is only half the engine. Creation without governed memory is possession (verified form enters a collective amnesia and is re-litigated, re-hallucinated, re-paid); memory without governed correction is mummification (the first wrong belief becomes permanent law).

V2 therefore specifies the **complete dual engine**:

> **The Creation Engine** turns chaos into verified form (V1, retained).
> **The Memory Engine** turns verified form into governed belief — admitted with provenance, contestable with evidence, revocable by calculated cascade, and never, ever edited.

> **Reliable Agent = Raw Generation + Harness (Structure) + The Scoped Human Subject**
> **Reliable Memory = Retrieval + Jurisdiction + Recall**

The two engines are the same fractal in two phases. **To create without remembering is possession; to remember without creating is mummification.** Neither closes the cycle alone.

## 2. The Three Emendations (What the Reference Taught V1)

### 2.1 Emendation I — The Subject is Also Contained

V1 made the human the constant `c` — the fixed, unvetoable point of the iteration. The reference breaks this as a paradigm: *human-in-the-loop as unquestionable root* is itself one of the possessions to be exorcised. The subject is sovereign over **intention, preference, risk acceptance, and value conflicts** — and epistemically impotent over everything else:

- The subject **cannot fabricate evidence** — cannot conjure an anchor, certify an unproven cell, or bless contamination. Refused, terminally, by design.
- **Consent, stale, is not consent.** Approval carries a time-to-live; a decision made on a world that has since moved is re-escalated, not honored.
- Authorization of the **irrelevant-to-repeat** is single-use. Replaying it is refused.
- Approval is **scoped**: an authorization from one context does not transfer to another.

> **The constant c carries its own temenos.** The subject vetoes the machine; the machine refuses the subject's shadows.

### 2.2 Emendation II — `VERIFY` ≠ `ADMIT`

V1 fused verification and integration into one station. The reference separates them, and the separation is the deepest insight of the synthesis:

- **Verification** confronts the concretized form with the admitted intent. It proves the thing *works*.
- **Admission** governs entry into the collective memory. It proves the thing *deserves to be believed* — anchor verifiable in the real, coverage closed, provenance chained, derivation acyclic. A successful act is not knowledge; operational success acquires no authority until verified *and then admitted*.

And admission demands its shadow named: every promotion must declare **what it excluded** — counted, typed, recorded — never discarded in silence. The integration that hides its exclusions is not admission; it is suppression.

### 2.3 Emendation III — Time Is a Coordinate, Not an Ambient Variable

V1's Fractal Law named time the only variable. The reference goes further: **time must be made queryable, not merely spent.** A monotonic sequence orders all admitted belief; every proposal and every approval declares the state it was based on. The consequences:

- **Belief has a timestamp.** "What did we hold to be true at moment N?" is always answerable, and remains answerable after correction.
- **Stale interpretation is refused.** Deciding from an outdated state without revalidating is a violation, not an optimization.
- **When the past is wrong and the moment of wronging is unknown, contamination is overestimated, never underestimated.** Worst-case by design.
- **Exhaustion never promotes.** A budget, a retry count, or a patience that runs out escalates — it never converts into belief. Fatigue is not evidence.

## 3. First Principles (Axioms of the Dual Engine)

1. **Axiom of Possession.** Ungoverned generation, however brilliant, presents its content as truth. No output is trustworthy because it *feels* true; it is trustworthy only because it *passed gates*. *(retained)*
2. **Axiom of Containment.** Chaos is not cured by suppression but by ritual — a bounded, repeatable sequence of transformations with verification between each stage. *(retained)*
3. **Axiom of Individuation.** Nothing reliable is ever *generated*. It is individuated — and, V2 adds, what is individuated is then *admitted*, not merely stored. *(amended)*
4. **Axiom of Prohibition.** Every function is defined as much by what it must not do as by what it does. The negative space is load-bearing. No sub-function may act as the whole. *(retained)*
5. **Axiom of Provenance.** Every proposition carries its epistemic status — validated, assumed, deferred, or unknown. An assumption marked as assumption is a hypothesis; an assumption presented as fact is a possession. No function may silently resolve an open question on behalf of the subject. *(retained)*
6. **Axiom of the Scoped Subject.** The human is the highest *intentional* authority at every level — and holds no *epistemic* privilege anywhere. The subject decides; the subject does not testify. Any process that cannot be interrupted or vetoed by the human is possessed; any evidence that exists only because the human blessed it is fabricated. *(amended by Emendation I)*
7. **Axiom of Fractal Self-Similarity.** The cycle is scale-invariant; only time constants differ. *(retained)*
8. **Axiom of Governed Belief.** Collective memory is a jurisdiction, not a cache. Entry requires admission with provenance; exit requires recall with evidence and calculated cascade. The persistent can be wrong — and being wrong is a governed, auditable, recoverable state. History is never rewritten; it is superseded, with a scar. *(new, from Emendation II)*
9. **Axiom of the Blind Gate.** The gate judges the content, never the speaker. The same content under any identity — friendly, hostile, senior, anonymous — receives the identical verdict. Authority of origin is a credential of submission, never of merit. *(new)*
10. **Axiom of Testimony.** No function verifies itself, and no function's self-report is evidence. Verification is read from the container's log, never from the agent's narration. Ego cannot audit ego — and ego's claim of having been audited is also not audit. *(new)*

## 4. The Engine: Ten Stations of Transformation

V1's nine stations, with Station 8 split into Verification and Admission (Emendation II), and Reflexion expanded into its governed-memory form:

```text
 1. CONCEPTION      The idea exists as formless potential — chaos with ambition.

 2. INTERROGATION   The idea confronts reality; projections are separated
                    from facts; open questions stay OPEN and visible.

 3. DIFFERENTIATION The idea is named, bounded, internally ordered, and
                    committed to falsifiability. To bound is to draw the mandala.

 4. SACRIFICE       The first incarnation must FAIL, visibly and honestly.
                    A test that cannot fail is not a test; it is a compliment.

 5. RESURRECTION    The minimal being that makes the failure articulate:
                    enough, and nothing more than enough.

 6. SUBLIMATION     Form purified, behavior preserved. Transformation
                    without regression.

 7. SHADOW-WORK     The idea meets the Other: the critic who questions instead
                    of solving, and the adversary whose function is disbelief.
                    "No issues found" is valid; a fabricated finding is the
                    worst failure mode. The adversary is blind to nothing —
                    the gate is blind only to the speaker.

 8. VERIFICATION    The concretized form is confronted with the admitted
                    intent. This proves the thing WORKS — and proves nothing
                    more. Operational success acquires no authority here.

 9. ADMISSION       The verified form requests entry into the collective
                    memory: anchor re-checkable in the real, coverage closed,
                    provenance chained, derivation acyclic — and the shadow of
                    the promotion NAMED: what was excluded, counted and typed.
                    The gate judges content, never speaker. An empty promotion
                    (everything excluded, honestly summarized) is a valid,
                    first-class outcome.

10. REFLEXION       The system records what happened (never what should have),
                    keeps testimony separate from the graph, answers "what did
                    we believe at moment N" for all N, and — when the persistent
                    is discovered wrong — RECALLS: a calculated, deterministic
                    cascade over the recorded derivation of belief, never a
                    human-curated edit. The old truth remains legible as the
                    belief of its time, marked with a scar. Rehabilitation is
                    cell-by-cell, by the normal proof path — no shortcuts,
                    no unrecall. The serpent turns, on evidence.
```

The cycle closes twice: **memory feeds conception; reflexion improves the engine and corrects the memory.**

3. **A refusal is recorded as a refusal** — with its typed reason and, where applicable, its client obligation. A refusal rendered as silent success is the gravest category of lie the system can tell.

## 6. The Fractal Law (Amended)

### 6.1 Self-Similarity Across Scales — Now Topological

V1 asserted the cycle repeats at every scale. The reference incarnates the scales as a **declared topology**: session → negotiation → transformation → micro-task → persistent memory. Each scale is a bounded temenos (a *horizon*) that runs the full station cycle internally, and **promotion crosses exactly one scale boundary at a time** — a submission that skips a boundary is refused. As above, so below — but strictly storey by storey.

### 6.2 Time as a First-Class Coordinate

Each scale owns its **budget ledger** — time, attempts, cost, and effect-classes of action — debited per action, audited at closure. The global monotonic sequence orders all admitted belief. The Fractal Law's "only variable" is therefore no longer implicit:

- **The iteration index (time) is recorded, ordered, and queryable** — staleness is detectable, worst-case contamination is computable, and historical belief is reconstructible.
- **The accumulator (memory) is governed** — admission, contestation, recall, supersession, each leaving the past legible.

### 6.3 The Two Currencies, Refined

- **Time dissipates** and is now *accountable per horizon*: budgets enforce that no scale spends beyond its means, and exhaustion always escalates, never promotes.
- **Memory accumulates** and is now *contestable per claim*: any admitted belief can be contested with evidence from any direction, at any scale — contestation travels, promotion does not skip.

**Technical debt** (V1) gains its V2 name: *stale base* — building on a world that has since moved. The system's answer is invariant: mark, revalidate, or refuse — never silently proceed.

### 7.2 Non-Functional Requirements (The Psychic Virtues, Second Edition)

All V1 virtues retained — **Humility, Provenance, Proportionality, Facticity, Reversibility, Minimalism, Self-knowledge** — amended and extended:

- **Scoped Sovereignty** — the subject's authority is intentional, not epistemic; consent decays; nothing irreversible is replayable.
- **Named Exclusion** — what is left out is counted, typed, and recorded; suppression is the shadow of integration and must be made visible.
- **Worst-Case Honesty** — when the record is uncertain, error bars widen; contamination is overestimated, never underestimated.
- **Blind Judgment** — verdicts are invariant to speaker identity, seniority, and friendliness.
- **Legible History** — every belief of every moment remains answerable; correction supersedes, never erases.
- **Typing of Refusal** — every refusal carries a machine-readable reason; a refusal is never rendered as silence or success.

## 8. Success Criteria

The dual engine succeeds when:

1. Chaos enters, verified and **admitted** form exits — passing every gate, with no archetype hosting its own judgment.
2. The human subject remains supreme in **intention** while holding no epistemic privilege anywhere — and the subject's own consent decays honestly with time and world-change.
3. Collective memory is a living jurisdiction: anything admitted can be contested with evidence, recalled by calculated cascade, and rehabilitated only by the normal proof path — and "what did we believe when we decided X" is always answerable.
4. The same cycle governs every scale, storey by storey — with time recorded as a coordinate, not merely spent.
5. The memory never lies, never floods — and never edits its past.

In sum: V2 succeeds when the Harness demonstrates, in silicon, the full depth-psychological law — that the path from chaos to creation runs through **naming, sacrifice, shadow-work, and admission**; that even the subject carries a temenos; that truth is not a state of the store but a **governed belief with a timestamp, a provenance, and a path for being wrong**; and that nothing reliable is ever *given* — only *earned through the cycle, and kept through its jurisdiction*.
