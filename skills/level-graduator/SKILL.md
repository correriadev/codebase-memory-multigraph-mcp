# Graduate procedures

**Status: deferred.** Graduation changes a universal procedure. A thematic version change belongs to Tradition's curators and requires re-verification by bound projects.

## Preconditions

Require a candidate with a documented human decision, a contract, a non-regression plan, and evidence for available host gates. When a predecessor exists, identify it and prepare a rollback plan that preserves its history. A novel skill may have no predecessor; do not invent one.

## Review

1. Identify valid predecessor outcomes, where a predecessor exists, and evidence that the candidate preserves them.
2. Check that the procedure preserves all three jurisdictions: project evidence for WHAT, operator-validated intent for WHY, and citable Tradition for HOW.
3. Check refusal of silent invention, thematic-graph writes, and self-approval. Any failure blocks the proposal.
4. Prepare a record linking candidate, evidence, operator decision, and rollback plan. Keep a predecessor's history legible.

The current CBM promotes horizons, not skill-contract versions. `promote_horizon` does not graduate a skill. The only current output is an operator review package or a documented block.
